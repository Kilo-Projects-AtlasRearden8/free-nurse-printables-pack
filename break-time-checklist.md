**Before your break**

- [ ] Notify charge nurse
- [ ] Break buddy assigned & aware of patients
- [ ] Quick check on assigned patients
- [ ] IV pumps set & alarms audible
- [ ] Call lights within reach
- [ ] Toileting offered / done
- [ ] Meds due before break given
- [ ] Report sheet / brain sheet updated
- [ ] Clock out

**During your break**

- [ ] Step away from the unit (if permitted)
- [ ] Eat something
- [ ] Drink water
- [ ] Bathroom
- [ ] Stretch / short walk
- [ ] Phone on silent / away
- [ ] Set alarm to return on time
